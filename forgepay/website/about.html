AboutForge Pay (Pty) Ltd.

FORGE,
explained.

A plain-language walkthrough of what we build, why it exists, and who it's for in the space of a coffee break.

01

What is FORGE?

FORGE is a financial infrastructure company. We build the Revenue Ontology a single canonical record that every payment, on every rail, gets normalized into. Cards, bank transfers, stablecoins, crypto, AI-agent micropayments and tokenized real-world assets all land in the same shape, with tax and reconciliation already worked out.

02

What problem does that solve?

Most companies run their money through five disconnected systems: a card processor, a crypto wallet, a tax tool bolted on after the fact, a billing platform, and a spreadsheet tying it all together. Every new payment method is another integration, another reconciliation job, another place for numbers to disagree.

FORGE replaces the five systems with one ledger. The payment rail becomes a parameter, not a decision.

03

Why does an AI agent need a credit bureau?

Autonomous agents already book travel, negotiate with suppliers, and pay for API calls usually by borrowing a human's card. That caps what they can do and keeps a person in every approval loop.

FORGE gives agents their own verifiable identity and a reputation built from their own transaction history so they can hold credit lines and negotiate terms on their own track record, not their parent company's balance sheet.

04

What does FORGE actually sell?

Five products that share one underlying ledger: Credit (agent identity & reputation), Payments (multi-rail checkout), Treasury (cash consolidation & yield), Custody (institutional key management), and Wallet (keyless wallets for people and agents). A company can start with any one of them and add the rest without re-platforming.

05

Where is FORGE based?

We're headquartered in Johannesburg, South Africa, building for a global market settlement doesn't care which continent a company is on.

06

Is FORGE hiring?

Slowly, on purpose. We're a small team working on hard, unglamorous infrastructure problems settlement, reconciliation, identity, credit risk. If that's your kind of work, write to us.
