Real yield.

FORGE's RWA registry tracks tokenized T-bills and money-market funds from issuers like BlackRock, Franklin Templeton, Ondo Finance, OpenEden and Superstate positions, income and redemptions, all in one place.

How it worksPositions, income, redemption

Held, accrued,
and redeemable.

The registry runs its own background jobs rather than relying on a manual reconciliation process.

NAV refresh

Runs every 6 hours to keep position valuations current.

Income accrual

Runs every 24 hours, distributing yield to every active position automatically.

Redemption flow

Positions can be redeemed back through the registry, with tax tracking carried through the same event.
