How it worksFive steps, every time

Policy first.
Signature last.

A transfer only reaches a signer after it has cleared every rule you've set. Nothing skips the line including the largest, most urgent request.

01

Policy check

Daily limits, destination whitelists, allowed hours, allowed chains evaluated before anything else happens. Any violation stops the request immediately, with a clear reason.

02

Sanctions screening

Every counterparty is screened. If the screening service is unreachable, the request is refused rather than waved through safe by default, never the other way around.

03

Multi-approver sign-off

Above your threshold, a transfer waits for named approvers in named roles e.g. one finance lead and one executive. No single person can approve alone.

04

Threshold signature

Once approved, the transaction is signed by a distributed signing process no single machine or person ever holds a complete key.

05

Settlement & audit

The signed transfer is broadcast and confirmed on-chain. Every step request, screen, approval, signature is written to a tamper-evident log that can never be edited or deleted.

ControlsBuilt for treasuries and funds

Governance,
not guesswork.

Every control a finance or security team asks for on day one configured once, enforced automatically on every transfer after.

Spending Limits

Daily aggregate caps per account. Attempts above the limit are rejected before a signature is requested.

Destination Whitelisting

Only pre-approved addresses can receive funds. Adding a new destination requires its own approval.

Role-Based Approval

Require specific roles not just any two people to sign off on large transfers.

Time-Window Controls

Restrict transfers to business hours in your timezone, or block them entirely outside approved windows.

Immutable Audit Log

Every request, approval, and signature is permanently recorded viewable by auditors, never editable by anyone.

Chain Restrictions

Limit which networks funds can move across. New chains are opt-in, never default-on.

InfrastructureWhat Custody runs on

Split seven ways.
Signed by four.

Custody is built on multi-party computation, not a vault with a password. A private key is never assembled in one place, at one time, by one party.

MPC threshold signing

Key material is split into seven shards. Four must cooperate to produce a signature. The full key is never reconstructed, so there is nothing to steal in one place.

Hardware-isolated shards

Each shard lives inside a hardware security module in a separate jurisdiction and cloud region. No operator, region, or provider outage can unilaterally move funds or freeze them.

Policy engine at the edge

Limits, whitelists, roles, time windows and sanctions screening are evaluated before a signing request is ever created. A non-compliant transfer is never signable.

Append-only ledger

The same canonical event store the rest of FORGE writes to. Every request, approval and signature is one immutable record auditors can read directly.

Independently reviewed

The signing ceremony and shard-recovery procedure are under external cryptographic review before general availability, not after it.

Recoverable by design

Losing shards below the threshold is survivable: remaining holders re-share to a fresh set without the underlying key ever being exposed.

CapabilitiesCustody that stays useful

Secured here.
Still working.

Most custody forces a trade-off: assets are safe but idle, or productive but exposed. Custody keeps holdings under your control while they trade, settle and earn.

Off-venue settlement

Trade on connected venues while assets stay in custody. Only the net result settles, so nothing sits on an exchange balance sheet waiting to be a counterparty risk.

Collateral mobility

Pledge custodied assets as margin without transferring them out. Positions open in minutes instead of waiting on a deposit confirmation.

Segregated accounts

Client assets are held in dedicated accounts, never pooled with FORGE's own balances or another client's. Segregation is enforced at the key layer, not by bookkeeping.

Staking in place

Earn protocol rewards on eligible assets without surrendering custody or handing keys to a third-party validator operator.

Venue connectivity

One custody account, many trading destinations. Add or remove venues without re-onboarding or moving assets between wallets.

Reporting for auditors

Position, transfer and approval reports export straight from the ledger, already reconciled against FORGE Treasury's view of the same funds.
