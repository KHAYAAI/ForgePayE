Built for people.
Ready for agents.

Same underlying wallet, same protections, same identity system whether a person signs in with a password or an autonomous agent authenticates on its own.

For people

Sign up. Transact. Done.

  • Email and password no seed phrase to write down or lose
  • Your key is encrypted and only ever unlocked in the moment you sign
  • Lost access? Recover through three trusted contacts, no support ticket needed
  • Send stablecoins, crypto, or fund purchases straight from your wallet
For agents

An identity an agent can act on.

  • Every agent gets its own wallet and its own verifiable digital identifier
  • Transaction history builds a reputation score other services can trust
  • Small payments settle instantly; anything large is routed to FORGE Custody automatically
  • Holds multiple currencies and rebalances itself so it always has what it needs
SecurityHow your key stays yours

We never see
your key in the clear.

Your private key is encrypted the moment it's created and stays encrypted at rest. It's only ever decrypted, in memory, for the instant it takes to sign then it's gone again.

Encrypted at Rest

Your key is locked with strong encryption derived from your password. No plaintext copy is ever stored or returned by any request.

Momentary Decryption

Signing a transaction briefly unlocks your key in memory, uses it once, and discards it immediately after.

Social Recovery

Name up to three trusted contacts. Any two can help you regain access if you're ever locked out no seed phrase required.

Verifiable Identity

Every wallet human or agent gets a decentralized identifier the rest of FORGE can check against, without exposing keys.

Automatic Tiering

Wallets are built to know their limits: amounts beyond consumer size are automatically handed off to FORGE Custody.

Every Action Logged

Every confirmed transaction is recorded once, in the same event log the rest of FORGE reads from nothing happens twice, nothing goes missing.
