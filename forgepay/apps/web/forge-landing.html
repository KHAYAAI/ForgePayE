IIIThe thesis
A new category

The log
is the moat.

Most businesses run payments out of five systems that don't speak to each other — a card processor, a tax tool, a billing engine, a crypto wallet, and a spreadsheet holding it together. Each integration is a tax you pay forever.

ForgePay inverts the stack. Every payment, on every rail, appends to one immutable, signed event log — the Revenue Ontology. We define the schema; the underlying networks become interchangeable. Routing intelligence, agent reputation and tax classification all compound on top of it.
