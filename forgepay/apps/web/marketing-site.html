Trusted Technology Stack

Payment Router

Hyperswitch (Rust) — Battle-tested payment routing engine. PCI vault, 99.99% uptime.

Billing Engine

Kill Bill (Java) — Subscription lifecycle management with automatic proration.

Treasury & OFAC

Python/FastAPI + Chainalysis — Real-time compliance screening and FX optimization.

On-Chain Reputation
