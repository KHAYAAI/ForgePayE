# ForgePay — FCA Payment Institution Licence Application Package

## Overview

This directory contains ForgePay's complete application package for authorisation as a **Payment Institution (PI)** under the **Payment Services Regulations 2017 (SI 2017/752)** ("PSR 2017"), supervised by the **Financial Conduct Authority (FCA)**.

ForgePay also requires separate **Cryptoasset Business Registration** under **The Money Laundering, Terrorist Financing and Transfer of Funds (Information on the Payer) Regulations 2017 (SI 2017/692)** ("MLR 2017") Regulation 14A for its stablecoin (USDC/USDT) and native crypto (BTC/ETH/LTC/XMR) payment services. That process is covered in document `10_fca_cryptoasset_registration.md`.

## Indicative Application Timeline

```
Month 0       Internal readiness audit; appoint legal counsel and compliance consultant
Month 1–2     Draft all application documents; appoint MLRO and Compliance Officer
Month 2       Submit PI application via FCA Connect
              Submit Cryptoasset Registration application (separate queue)
Month 2–8     FCA review period (statutory target: 3 months from complete application,
              but FCA routinely takes 6–12 months in practice as at 2025–2026)
Month 3–8     FCA case officer queries — respond within FCA-set deadlines (typically 20 BD)
Month 8–14    FCA decision — grant, conditional grant, or refusal
              Cryptoasset Registration: 12+ months typical (high rejection rate ~80%)
Month 14+     Authorisation effective — commence regulated payment services
```

> **Note:** The FCA's target determination period is 3 months from receipt of a **complete** application (PSR 2017 reg 10). Applications are rarely deemed complete on first submission. Budget 9–18 months end-to-end.

## FCA Connect Portal — Step-by-Step Instructions

### 1. Pre-Application Preparation

1. Register for **FCA Connect** at https://connect.fca.org.uk/
2. Complete a **Pre-Application Meeting (PAM)** request via Connect — strongly recommended for novel business models (crypto payments, x402 protocol). Allows early FCA feedback before committing application fees.
3. Review FCA's **Payment Services and E-Money approach document** and **FG17/15** (approach to PI/EMI authorisation).
4. Confirm entity type: ForgePay must be incorporated as a **UK private limited company** before submitting.

### 2. Application Fee

| Application Type | Fee (2025/26) |
|---|---|
| Payment Institution (PI) | £5,000 (non-refundable) |
| Small Payment Institution (SPI) | £1,500 |
| Cryptoasset Business Registration | £10,000 (applications up to 5 crypto activities) |

> ForgePay intends to apply as a full PI (not SPI) given projected payment volumes exceeding the SPI threshold (€3m per month average over 12 months).

### 3. FCA Connect Form Sections

Complete the following sections in FCA Connect (PI application form):

| Section | Description | Supporting Document |
|---|---|---|
| A | Applicant details | Company registration, Articles of Association |
| B | Programme of Operations | `02_programme_of_operations.md` |
| C | Business Plan | `03_regulatory_business_plan.md` |
| D | Financial Projections | `04_financial_projections.md` |
| E | Evidence of initial capital | Bank statement (€125,000 minimum) |
| F | Description of governance arrangements | `08_smf_declarations.md` |
| G | Description of internal control mechanisms | `07_aml_policy.md`, `09_operational_resilience_policy.md` |
| H | Description of safeguarding arrangements | `05_safeguarding_policy.md` |
| I | Description of wind-down arrangements | `06_wind_down_plan.md` |

| K | SMF applications (individual applications) | `08_smf_declarations.md` |
| L | Regulatory references | `08_smf_declarations.md` |

### 4. Key FCA Contacts

- **FCA Authorisations**: authorisations.applications@fca.org.uk
- **FCA Supervision (Payments)**: supervision.payments@fca.org.uk
- **FCA Cryptoassets**: cryptoasset.registration@fca.org.uk
- **FCA Consumer Helpline** (regulatory queries): 0800 111 6768

### 5. Post-Submission

- Monitor FCA Connect for queries — the FCA will correspond via Connect
- Respond to all queries within the deadline stated (failure to respond can result in refusal)
- The FCA may conduct a **site visit** or **management interviews** before granting authorisation
- Notify FCA immediately of any **material changes** to the application during review
