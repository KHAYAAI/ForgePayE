## 1. Introduction to SM&CR for Payment Institutions

The **Senior Managers and Certification Regime (SM&CR)** applies to FCA-authorised Payment Institutions under FSMA 2000 (as extended by the Bank of England and Financial Services Act 2016). Under SM&CR:

- **Senior Manager Functions (SMFs):** Specified roles requiring prior FCA approval; individuals must be assessed as fit and proper
- **Certification Regime:** Roles that are significant to the firm's operations but not SMFs; certified annually by the firm
- **Conduct Rules:** Apply to all staff in scope; set baseline standards of individual conduct

This document provides:
- The SMF roles required for ForgePay as a Payment Institution
- Template Statements of Responsibilities (SoR) for each SMF
- Individual Questionnaire (IQ) completion guide
- Regulatory References procedure

## 4. Individual Questionnaire (IQ) — Completion Guide

All SMF applicants must complete the FCA **Individual Questionnaire (IQ)** via FCA Connect. The IQ assesses fitness and propriety under three headings:
1. **Honesty, integrity, and reputation** (criminal convictions, civil proceedings, regulatory history)
2. **Competence and capability** (qualifications, experience, training)
3. **Financial soundness** (insolvency, bankruptcy, County Court Judgements)

### 4.1 IQ Sections

| IQ Section | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | Personal details (name, address, nationality, date of birth, NI number) |
| 2 | Employment history (all roles within last 10 years) |
| 3 | Regulatory history (previous FCA/PRA approvals, withdrawals, refusals) |
| 4 | Criminal convictions (UK and overseas — self-declaration) |
| 5 | Civil proceedings (judgements against the individual) |
| 6 | Fitness and propriety — general (any matters relevant to F&P not covered above) |
| 7 | Declaration and signature |

### 4.2 Key IQ Guidance Points

- **Answer all questions fully:** Failure to disclose a matter, even if you consider it irrelevant, can result in refusal and/or enforcement action for misleading the regulator
- **Disclose overseas matters:** Criminal proceedings and regulatory actions in other jurisdictions must be disclosed
- **Previous SMF refusals:** Any previous refusal of FCA approval is a serious adverse factor and must be disclosed; seek legal advice on presentation
- **Spent convictions:** The FCA can consider spent convictions under the Rehabilitation of Offenders Act 1974 for fitness and propriety purposes — disclose and seek legal advice
- **IQ is a living document:** If circumstances change during the FCA's review period, the IQ must be updated

### 4.3 Supporting Documents for IQ

Attach the following to each IQ in FCA Connect:
- Curriculum vitae (CV) — detailed, covering all roles in last 10 years with dates, employer names, reporting lines, and responsibilities
- Professional qualifications certificates (original or certified copies)
- DBS (Disclosure and Barring Service) basic check certificate (dated within 3 months)
- For non-UK nationals: equivalent criminal record check from country of residence/citizenship
- Reference letters from most recent 2 employers (separate from FCA Regulatory References)

## 5. Regulatory References

### 5.1 Obligation to Request

Under FCA rules (SYSC 22), ForgePay must take reasonable steps to obtain a **Regulatory Reference** from each employer where an SMF applicant has held:
- An FCA-controlled function or SMF
- A role subject to the Certification Regime
- A PRA-controlled function

within the **last 6 years**.

### 5.3 Regulatory Reference Provision (Outgoing)

ForgePay will provide Regulatory References for its own past employees / SMF holders upon request from a prospective employer, in accordance with SYSC 22. This obligation persists for 6 years after the individual leaves ForgePay.

ForgePay maintains a Regulatory Reference Register recording:
- Individuals for whom we may need to provide references
- Any disclosable matters relating to those individuals
- References provided (recipient, date, content summary)

### 5.4 Handling Adverse References

If a regulatory reference received contains adverse information:
1. ForgePay's Compliance Officer reviews the reference
2. The applicant is given the opportunity to respond (SYSC 22.5)
3. The MLRO and Compliance Officer assess materiality and impact on F&P
4. If materially adverse: the Board decides whether to proceed with appointment
5. Any materially adverse matter is disclosed to the FCA proactively (do not wait for FCA to ask)
6. FCA is notified immediately if adverse information comes to light after submission of the IQ

## 6. FCA Approved Persons and SMF Approval Process

### 6.1 Timeline

FCA approval for each SMF holder is sought concurrently with the PI application:

| Step | Timeline |
|---|---|
| Submit PI application (firm) | Month 0 |
| Submit individual IQs for each SMF applicant | Month 0 (simultaneously with firm application) |
| FCA review of individual F&P | Month 0–3+ (runs in parallel with firm review) |
| FCA may request interview with SMF applicants | During review period |
| FCA decision on individuals | At or before firm authorisation decision |

### 6.2 Interim Arrangements

If ForgePay requires an MLRO or Compliance Officer to be in post before FCA approval is granted (e.g., for operational readiness), ForgePay must ensure the individual is clearly acting in a non-regulated capacity until FCA approval is received, or obtain written FCA confirmation that interim arrangements are acceptable.

### 6.3 Ongoing F&P Assessment

ForgePay will conduct annual fitness and propriety assessments of all SMF holders. The assessment covers:
- Updated criminal record check (DBS — every 3 years or upon trigger event)
- Updated adverse media and regulatory action check
- Self-declaration of any changes in circumstances
- Review of performance and conduct during the year

Results are recorded and available for FCA review.
