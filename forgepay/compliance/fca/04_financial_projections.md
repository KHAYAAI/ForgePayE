## 5. Capital Adequacy Calculations

PSR 2017 Schedule 3 sets out three methods for calculating the minimum capital requirement for a Payment Institution. ForgePay must comply with the **highest** of:
- Method A: Fixed minimum of €125,000 (for institutions providing services in Schedule 1 paragraphs 1–5)
- Method B: Fixed Overhead Requirement (FOR) = 25% of the previous year's fixed overheads
- Method C: Payment volume method (percentage of payment volume)

### 5.1 Method A — Fixed Minimum

€125,000 = approximately **£108,695** at 1.15 GBP/EUR exchange rate.

ForgePay will maintain capital of at least this amount from inception.

### 5.2 Method B — Fixed Overhead Requirement (FOR)

The FOR is calculated as **25% of fixed overheads** (as defined in PSR 2017 Schedule 3 para 3).

Fixed overheads are total expenditure less:
- Variable costs directly linked to payment volume
- Discretionary bonuses
- Exceptional one-off items

## 8. Regulatory Capital — Monitoring and Reporting

ForgePay will implement monthly capital adequacy monitoring:

1. **Monthly calculation:** CFO calculates own funds and required minimum at month-end
2. **Reporting:** Capital adequacy summary included in monthly board report
3. **Breach trigger:** If own funds fall within 10% of regulatory minimum, immediate board escalation
4. **FCA notification:** FCA notified within 3 business days if own funds breach regulatory minimum (PSR 2017 reg 29)
5. **Annual FCA return:** Capital adequacy reported in FCA RegData annual return
