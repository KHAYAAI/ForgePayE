# ForgePay — FSCA License Application Submission Guide

**Document Classification:** Internal Use
**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** 2026-06-25
**Prepared By:** Chief Compliance Officer
**Purpose:** Step-by-step guide for submitting ForgePay's FSCA PSP license application

---

## Overview

This guide walks the Chief Compliance Officer and appointed external legal counsel through the complete process of submitting ForgePay's FSCA Payment Service Provider license application — from portal registration through to final license decision. Do not submit until all checklist items in `01_application_checklist.md` are complete or have been deliberately excluded with documented rationale.

**Estimated submission preparation time:** 8–12 weeks from commencement of document compilation.
**Estimated FSCA processing time:** 10–14 months from submission.
**Application fees:** R 50,000–R 150,000 (see Section 4).

---

## Phase 1: Pre-Submission Preparation (Weeks 1–10)

### Step 1.1: Engage External Legal Counsel and Compliance Consultant

Before preparing any documents:
- Appoint a South African attorney admitted to practice with experience in FSCA financial services licensing
- Recommended: firms with dedicated financial services regulation practices (verify current FSCA licensing experience)
- Appoint a compliance consultant registered with the Compliance Institute of Southern Africa (CISA) to review the AML/CFT Policy and compliance programme
- Agree on scope: document review, regulatory correspondence, FSCA liaison, and representation at any hearings

**Contact FSCA Licensing Unit for pre-application meeting:**
- Email: licensing@fsca.org.za
- Phone: +27 12 428 8000
- Request: Pre-application consultation meeting to confirm license category, documents required, and fee schedule
- FSCA typically holds pre-application meetings with prospective applicants — this is highly recommended

### Step 1.2: Confirm License Category

ForgePay's activities may fall under one or more FSCA authorisation categories:

| Activity | Likely Category | Confirm with FSCA |
|---|---|---|
| Money transmission / fund holding | Money Transmitter (Category II or III FSP) | Yes |
| Merchant of Record services | Could require additional authorisation | Yes |
| Crypto-asset services (BTC/ETH/LTC/XMR) | CASP (Crypto Asset Service Provider) under amended FAIS Act | Confirm separately |
| USDC/USDT stablecoin settlement | May fall under PSP or CASP | Confirm |
| x402 AI agent payments | Novel; FSCA guidance required | Confirm |

**Action:** At the pre-application meeting, ask the FSCA to confirm:
1. The exact license category code for ForgePay's business model
2. Whether crypto and stablecoin services require a separate CASP license
3. Whether the MoR model requires additional authorisation beyond PSP
4. The current minimum capital requirement for ForgePay's category

### Step 1.3: Register as FIC Accountable Institution

FIC registration runs concurrently with the FSCA application. Do not delay:
1. Visit goAML web portal: [https://goaml.fic.gov.za](https://goaml.fic.gov.za)
2. Register ForgePay as an Accountable Institution under Schedule 1, Item 22 (money remitter / payment service provider)
3. Designate the AMLCO as the reporting officer
4. Obtain FIC registration number
5. Include FIC registration confirmation in FSCA application package (Section D of checklist)

### Step 1.4: Register Information Officer with Information Regulator (POPIA)

1. Visit Information Regulator portal: [https://www.inforegulator.org.za](https://www.inforegulator.org.za)
2. Register ForgePay's Information Officer
3. Obtain registration confirmation
4. Include confirmation in POPIA compliance documentation

### Step 1.5: Complete All Checklist Documents

Work through `01_application_checklist.md` section by section:
- Assign each item to a responsible owner with a deadline
- Track status weekly in a project management tool
- Do not proceed to submission until all mandatory items are complete

### Step 1.6: Internal Review

Before external review:
1. CCO reviews all documents for internal consistency
2. CFO signs off on all financial documents
3. CTO signs off on IT and systems documents
4. All director fit and proper declarations completed and commissioned

### Step 1.7: External Legal Review

Allow at least 2 weeks for external counsel to:
- Review every document in the package
- Check consistency with current FSCA requirements (FSCA conduct standards and guidance notes may change)
- Review the AML/CFT Policy against FIC Act and FIC guidance notes
- Review financial projections for regulatory adequacy
- Advise on any missing items or weaknesses in the application

Incorporate all legal counsel feedback before final sign-off.

## Phase 2: FSCA Online Portal — Registration and Login

### Step 2.1: Access the FSCA Online Portal

- **Portal URL:** [https://fsp.fsca.org.za](https://fsp.fsca.org.za) (confirm current URL with FSCA — may change)
- **Alternative:** Some FSCA applications are submitted via the FSCA's general online portal at [https://www.fsca.org.za/Regulated%20Entities/Pages/Apply-for-an-FSP-licence.aspx](https://www.fsca.org.za/Regulated%20Entities/Pages/Apply-for-an-FSP-licence.aspx)
- **Enquiries for portal access:** licensing@fsca.org.za

### Step 2.2: Create an Applicant Account

1. Navigate to the FSCA licensing portal
2. Select "New Application" or "Register as Applicant"
3. Complete entity registration:
   - Company registration number (CIPC)
   - Entity name (exactly as per CIPC records)
   - Registered address
   - Contact details (CCO name, email, phone)
4. Receive confirmation email with login credentials
5. Log in and familiarise yourself with the portal interface before submission day

**Note:** FSCA portals may time out during lengthy data entry sessions. Prepare all data offline and paste into the portal to minimise session issues.

### Step 2.3: Select License Category

Within the portal:
1. Select "Financial Services Provider License Application"
2. Select the applicable license category (confirmed at pre-application meeting — likely Category II or III FSP, or Money Transmitter)
3. Note the category code — all subsequent documents should reference this code

### Step 4.1: Fee Structure

FSCA licensing fees vary based on license category and are subject to annual adjustment. The following is the expected fee range as at 2026:

| Fee Type | Amount (ZAR) | When Payable |
|---|---|---|
| Application fee (non-refundable) | R 50,000–R 150,000 | At submission |

**Note:** Fees are **non-refundable** regardless of the outcome of the application. Confirm the exact fee applicable to ForgePay's license category with the FSCA before payment.

3. Retain proof of payment

## Phase 5: Post-Submission — What to Expect

### Step 5.1: FSCA Intake Review (Month 1–2 after submission)

**What happens:**
- FSCA licensing team reviews the application for completeness
- If documents are missing or unclear, FSCA will issue a "deficiency notice" requesting additional information
- Respond to deficiency notices promptly (within the timeframe specified by FSCA, typically 30–60 days)
- Failure to respond to deficiencies may result in the application being deemed withdrawn

**ForgePay actions:**
- Assign CCO as primary FSCA correspondent
- Log all FSCA correspondence (email, letters, portal messages) centrally
- Respond to deficiency notices via the portal and simultaneously by email to the FSCA case officer

### Step 5.2: Fit and Proper Assessment (Month 2–4)

**What happens:**
- FSCA assessors review Fit and Proper declarations for all directors, key persons, and shareholders
- Background checks are conducted (CIPC, credit bureau, regulatory databases, police records)
- FSCA may request additional information or clarifications from individual persons
- FSCA may contact previous regulators or employers

**ForgePay actions:**
- Notify all directors and key persons to expect FSCA contact
- Ensure all persons are available and responsive to FSCA queries
- If a director has a complex regulatory history (e.g., prior enforcement action), engage legal counsel to prepare a detailed written explanation in advance

**Common delay factors:**
- Incomplete CVs (gaps in employment history)
- Credit bureau issues (unsatisfied judgments, adverse listings)
- Foreign clearance certificates taking time to obtain
- Prior regulatory history requiring detailed explanation

### Step 5.3: Policy and Business Plan Review (Month 3–6)

**What happens:**
- FSCA assessors review the AML/CFT Policy, IT Security Policy, business plan, and financial projections
- Assessors may request clarification on specific policies or additional documentation
- FSCA may request that certain policies be strengthened or expanded

**ForgePay actions:**
- AMLCO and CCO should be available to provide written responses and attend any review meetings
- If FSCA requests policy amendments, implement promptly and resubmit the revised version with a version change log
- Track all policy version changes in Git with commit messages referencing FSCA correspondence

**Likely FSCA focus areas for ForgePay:**
- AML/CFT controls for crypto (XMR especially — expect detailed scrutiny)
- x402 AI agent payment controls (novel; FSCA may request additional safeguards)
- Blockchain analytics integration (confirm vendor name and contract)
- Cross-border stablecoin settlement and SARB exchange control compliance
- Data residency (POPIA af-south-1 commitment)

### Step 5.4: On-Site or Virtual Systems Inspection (Month 6–10)

**What happens:**
- FSCA may conduct an on-site visit or virtual inspection of ForgePay's systems and operations
- Inspectors will want to see: actual systems functioning, access controls, audit logs, client funds account, AML monitoring in action
- CTO and CCO must be present; prepare a demo environment

**Preparation:**
- [ ] Prepare a system demonstration script showing: payment flow from merchant onboarding → transaction → tokenization → settlement
- [ ] Demonstrate AML rule engine: show how rules AML-001 through AML-008 fire and are triaged
- [ ] Show OFAC/sanctions screening: demonstrate a test transaction against a flagged address
- [ ] Show client funds trust account: bank balance reconciliation with platform ledger
- [ ] Show access controls: RBAC matrix; MFA login demonstration; break-glass log
- [ ] Show audit logs: OTEL traces and PostgreSQL audit_events for a test transaction
- [ ] Show Business Continuity Plan: evidence of last BCP test
- [ ] Have all board resolutions and policies available in physical form

### Step 5.5: FSCA Clarification Period (Ongoing during review)

FSCA may issue requests for information (RFIs) at any stage. Each RFI must be:
1. Logged in the compliance project tracker with receipt date and response deadline
2. Reviewed by CCO and legal counsel before responding
3. Responded to within the FSCA's specified timeframe (typically 30 days)
4. Uploaded via the portal with simultaneous email to case officer

**Do not miss RFI deadlines.** Late responses can restart the review clock or result in adverse decisions.

### Step 5.6: License Decision (Month 10–14)

**Outcomes:**
- **Granted:** FSCA issues a license with specific conditions. Carefully review all conditions; some may require operational changes or ongoing reporting.
- **Conditional grant:** FSCA grants the license subject to specific conditions being met within a defined period (e.g., appoint additional compliance staff, obtain additional insurance, complete a specific audit within 6 months).
- **Refused:** FSCA provides reasons for refusal in writing. ForgePay may appeal to the FSCA or the Financial Services Tribunal within the prescribed period. Engage legal counsel immediately on receipt of a refusal.
- **Deemed withdrawn:** FSCA may deem the application withdrawn if ForgePay fails to respond to deficiencies within the required period.

### Step 5.7: On Receipt of License

1. Obtain the original license certificate from FSCA
2. Verify that all license conditions are recorded correctly
3. Publish the FSP license number on ForgePay's website and in merchant agreements (required by FAIS Act)
4. Set up calendar reminders for all periodic license obligations (annual returns, fee payments, reporting)
5. Brief all staff on post-license compliance obligations
6. Notify key partners (acquirers, card schemes) of FSCA license number

## Phase 6: Post-License Obligations

### 6.1 Ongoing FSCA Obligations

Once licensed, ForgePay must:

| Obligation | Frequency | Deadline | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Annual statutory return to FSCA | Annual | Within 4 months of financial year end | CCO |
| Audited financial statements (if required by license) | Annual | As per license conditions | CFO |
| Notification of material changes (directors, key persons, address, shareholding) | On change (promptly) | Without undue delay | CCO |
| Notification of material incidents (breach, regulatory action, liquidity concern) | On occurrence | Immediately | CEO / CCO |
| Compliance officer annual fit and proper renewal | Annual | Per FSCA schedule | CCO |
| FSCA license fee payment | Annual | Per FSCA invoice | CFO |
| Policy reviews and board approval | Annual | Per board calendar | CCO |
| External compliance audit | Annual | Within 6 months of financial year end | CCO |
| AML/CFT programme review (BWRA update) | Annual | Per AMLCO schedule | AMLCO |
| FIC Accountable Institution annual confirmation | Annual | Per FIC schedule | AMLCO |
| PCI DSS assessment | Annual (or per scheme requirement) | Per PCI schedule | CTO |
| External penetration test | Annual | Within 12 months of previous test | CTO |

### 6.2 FSCA Conduct Obligations

Post-license, ForgePay must comply with all applicable FSCA Conduct Standards, including:
- Treating Customers Fairly (TCF) framework
- Complaints handling procedure
- Advertising and marketing standards (no misleading representations)
- Consumer disclosure requirements (fees, terms, dispute resolution)
- Conflicts of interest management

### 6.3 Record Retention

All application documents, FSCA correspondence, and compliance records must be retained for at least 5 years from the date of license grant (or longer if required by other legislation).

Store all records in ForgePay's compliance document management system with appropriate access controls.
