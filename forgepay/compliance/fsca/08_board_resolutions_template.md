## Resolution 1: Authority to Apply for FSCA Payment Service Provider License

**WHEREAS:**

1. ForgePay (Pty) Ltd ("the Company") operates a payment orchestration platform serving merchants in the Republic of South Africa.

2. The Company provides payment services including card processing, bank transfer (EFT), stablecoin settlement (USDC/USDT), cryptocurrency payments (BTC/ETH/LTC/XMR), and AI agent payments (x402 protocol).

3. The Financial Sector Conduct Authority (FSCA) requires Payment Service Providers operating in South Africa to hold a valid Payment Service Provider license in terms of the Financial Sector Regulation Act 9 of 2017 (FSRA) and applicable conduct standards.

4. The Board has considered the legal and regulatory requirements and is satisfied that it is in the best interests of the Company to apply for the FSCA Payment Service Provider license.

**NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED:**

## Resolution 5: Adoption of IT Security Policy and Systems and Controls Framework

**WHEREAS:**

1. The FSCA requires Payment Service Providers to maintain robust IT systems and operational controls commensurate with their risk profile and the sensitivity of data they process.

2. ForgePay processes cardholder data, personal information of merchants and consumers, and sensitive financial transaction data, requiring strong information security controls.

3. The Board has reviewed the IT Systems and Operational Controls document prepared by the Chief Technology Officer (Version 1.0, dated [Date]), a copy of which is attached hereto as Annexure C.

4. The Board is satisfied that the IT Security Policy and Systems and Controls Framework is appropriate and meets PCI DSS, POPIA, and FSCA requirements.

**NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED:**

1. **THAT** the IT Systems and Operational Controls document (Version 1.0, dated [Date]) and the IT Security Policy embedded therein are hereby adopted as the Company's official IT Security Policy with effect from [Date].

2. **THAT** the Chief Technology Officer is directed to implement and maintain all technical controls described in the IT Security Policy, including:
   - PCI DSS compliance for cardholder data (Hyperswitch tokenization vault)
   - Encryption of all data in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256)
   - Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) and Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) for all system access
   - Annual external penetration testing by a CREST-accredited firm
   - Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery plans, tested annually
   - Incident Response Plan, with defined breach notification procedures (72-hour POPIA rule)
   - Audit logging via OpenTelemetry and PostgreSQL audit tables, with 5-year retention
   - All primary data and processing hosted in AWS af-south-1 (Cape Town) to comply with POPIA data residency requirements

3. **THAT** the IT Security Policy shall be reviewed at least annually by the Chief Technology Officer in conjunction with the Chief Compliance Officer, and material amendments shall be brought to the Board for approval.

4. **THAT** the Chief Technology Officer shall report to the Board quarterly on IT security posture, including any material incidents, vulnerability findings, or changes to the systems architecture.
