# ForgePay Ongoing Compliance Calendar
## Annual Compliance Deadlines and Recurring Obligations

**Owner:** Chief Compliance Officer
**Review Frequency:** Updated annually; maintained in real-time
**Version:** 1.0 (June 2026)

---

## Overview

This calendar documents all recurring compliance obligations for ForgePay as a federally registered MSB and holder of state Money Transmitter Licenses. It is organized by frequency and then by month. The CCO is responsible for ensuring all deadlines are met; the compliance team maintains a live version of this calendar in the compliance case management system.

**How to use this calendar:**
1. At the start of each month, review the "Monthly Obligations" section
2. At the start of each quarter, add the quarterly obligations to the team's task tracker
3. Review the annual calendar at the start of each year and confirm all due dates with current state requirements (deadlines shift when they fall on weekends/holidays)

## FinCEN MSB Re-Registration Schedule

**Requirement:** 31 CFR 1022.380(b)(2) — Re-register every two years by December 31

| Year | Action | Deadline |
|------|--------|----------|
| 2026 | Initial registration (within 180 days of launch) | By launch + 180 days |
| 2028 | First re-registration | December 31, 2028 |
| 2030 | Second re-registration | December 31, 2030 |
| 2032 | Third re-registration | December 31, 2032 |

Set a calendar reminder in October of each re-registration year to begin the re-registration process.

## Incident and Escalation Triggers

The following events trigger immediate CCO action outside the normal calendar cycle:

| Event | Required Action | Deadline |
|-------|-----------------|---------|
| OFAC SDN emergency designation (terrorist financing-related) | Review all existing customers against new designation; block matches | Same day |
| SAR detected requiring 314(a) follow-up | Respond through 314(a) portal | Within 14 business days |
| Potential OFAC violation discovered | Consult legal counsel re: voluntary disclosure | Immediate |
| State exam notice received | Form exam response team; begin document preparation | Within 5 business days |
| FinCEN enforcement action relevant to ForgePay's business | Review program; update as needed; brief Board | Within 30 days |
| Data breach involving customer financial data | State notification (varies — typically 30–72 hours) | Per state breach notification law |
| Material change to ForgePay business model | Update FinCEN registration; notify state regulators | Per state/FinCEN requirements |
| Change in control (10%+ ownership change) | Prior NMLS/state/NYDFS approval as required | Before transaction closes |
| CCO resignation or termination | Appoint interim CCO; notify states with immediate change requirements | Within required timeframe per state |

## Compliance Calendar Maintenance

This calendar must be:
- Reviewed and confirmed accurate at the start of each year (January)
- Updated within 30 days of any regulatory change affecting a deadline
- Maintained as a shared team document (not just in one person's head or personal calendar)
- Cross-referenced with the compliance case management system for task tracking

The CCO is responsible for maintaining this calendar and for ensuring all deadlines are met. Failure to meet regulatory deadlines is itself a compliance violation and can result in penalties, license suspension, or revocation.
