## Overview

ForgePay operates as a payment orchestration platform processing card payments, USDC/USDT stablecoins, cryptocurrency (BTC/ETH/LTC/XMR), and AI agent payments (x402 protocol) in the United States. This activity triggers federal and state money transmission licensing requirements.

**Primary federal regulator:** Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN), U.S. Department of the Treasury
**State licensing system:** Nationwide Multistate Licensing System (NMLS)
**Additional NY regulator:** New York Department of Financial Services (NYDFS) — BitLicense required for virtual currency activity

---

## Why ForgePay Requires MSB Registration and MTLs

Under 31 U.S.C. § 5330 and 31 CFR Part 1022, any person engaged in money transmission as a business must:

1. Register with FinCEN as a Money Services Business (MSB) within 180 days of starting operations
2. Obtain a Money Transmitter License in each state where it operates (before commencing operations in that state)
3. Implement a written Bank Secrecy Act (BSA) / Anti-Money Laundering (AML) compliance program

ForgePay's activities that trigger these requirements:
- **Money transmission:** Moving funds on behalf of merchants and consumers
- **Dealer in foreign exchange / virtual currency:** Processing USDC, USDT, BTC, ETH, LTC, XMR
- **Payment intermediary:** Accepting and transmitting value through the x402 AI agent payment protocol

## NMLS Overview

The **Nationwide Multistate Licensing System (NMLS)** at [https://www.nmlsconsumeraccess.org/](https://www.nmlsconsumeraccess.org/) is the centralized licensing portal for most states. Key points:

- **Company form:** MU1 — filed once; state-specific amendments attach to the base filing
- **Control person form:** MU2 — required for each officer, director, and 10%+ owner
- **Surety bond:** Most states require a surety bond uploaded in NMLS; amounts range from $25,000 (small states) to $1,000,000+ (NY, CA)
- **Renewals:** Most licenses renew annually through NMLS in November–December
- **NMLS Company ID:** Assigned upon account creation; used on all state applications

**NMLS Resource Center for Money Transmitters:** [https://mortgage.nationwidelicensingsystem.org/slr/Pages/msb.aspx](https://mortgage.nationwidelicensingsystem.org/slr/Pages/msb.aspx)
