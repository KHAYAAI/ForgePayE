# State Money Transmitter License (MTL) Priority Matrix

**Purpose:** State-by-state analysis of money transmitter licensing requirements for ForgePay
**Last Updated:** June 2026
**Note:** All states require MTL before commencing money transmission operations unless a specific exemption applies. Fees, bond amounts, and timelines are subject to change — verify with current state statutes and NMLS checklists.

---

## How to Read This Matrix

- **Regulator:** State agency that issues the MTL
- **Portal:** Where to apply (most states use NMLS; some have state-specific portals)
- **Surety Bond:** Minimum bond amount required (some states scale with volume)
- **Net Worth:** Minimum net worth requirement
- **Timeline:** Estimated processing time from complete application
- **Annual Fee:** License fee (initial and/or annual renewal)
- **NMLS:** Whether the state uses NMLS for this license

## Tier 1 — Highest Priority States
*File applications immediately; highest user concentration and strictest requirements*

---

### New York — HIGHEST PRIORITY

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Regulator** | New York Department of Financial Services (NYDFS) |
| **MTL Statute** | New York Banking Law Article XIII-B |
| **Portal** | NMLS (Money Transmitter license) + NYDFS separate BitLicense application |
| **Surety Bond** | $500,000 minimum (NYDFS may require more based on volume) |
| **Net Worth** | $500,000 minimum |
| **Initial Application Fee** | $5,000 (MTL); $5,000 + investigation costs (BitLicense — see below) |
| **Annual Renewal Fee** | $5,000 (MTL); $5,000 (BitLicense) |
| **Timeline** | MTL: 12–18 months; BitLicense: 12–24 months |
| **NMLS** | Yes (MTL); No (BitLicense — separate NYDFS application) |

**Key Requirements:**
- Two-year audited financial statements
- Background checks on all principals and 10%+ shareholders
- AML/cybersecurity programs submitted and reviewed by NYDFS
- Dedicated New York compliance officer
- Cybersecurity program compliant with 23 NYCRR Part 500
- Capital adequate to cover in-transit customer funds

**BitLicense Note:**
ForgePay's USDC, USDT, BTC, ETH, LTC, and XMR activities require a **separate** NY BitLicense under 23 NYCRR Part 200. The BitLicense covers "virtual currency business activity" including transmitting, storing, controlling, buying, selling, or exchanging virtual currency. See `05_ny_bitlicense_package.md` for full details.

**Strategy:** File MTL and BitLicense applications simultaneously. Do not offer services to New York residents until both licenses are obtained. A NYDFS "no-action" letter or limited purpose charter may be sought as an interim measure.

### California

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Regulator** | California Department of Financial Protection and Innovation (DFPI) |
| **MTL Statute** | California Money Transmission Act (Cal. Fin. Code § 2000 et seq.) |
| **Portal** | NMLS |
| **Surety Bond** | $250,000 minimum; scales with annual dollar volume up to $7,000,000 |
| **Net Worth** | $500,000 minimum tangible net worth |
| **Initial Application Fee** | $5,000 |
| **Annual Renewal Fee** | $2,500 |
| **Timeline** | 6–12 months |
| **NMLS** | Yes |

**Key Requirements:**
- Audited financial statements (2 years)
- Business plan including 3-year financial projections
- AML program submission
- Background checks on all control persons
- Net worth maintained throughout licensure
- Quarterly and annual financial reports to DFPI
- Permissible investment requirement: customer funds must be backed 1:1 by permissible investments (US Treasuries, agency securities, FDIC-insured deposits)

**Crypto Note:** DFPI issued guidance that crypto exchanges need MTL; assess whether DFPI's Digital Financial Assets Law (DFAL, effective July 2025) requires a separate license for ForgePay's virtual currency activities.

## Key Cross-State Considerations

### Permissible Investments
Most states require ForgePay to hold "permissible investments" equal to 100% of outstanding money transmission obligations. Permissible investments typically include:
- Cash and FDIC-insured bank deposits
- U.S. Treasury securities
- Agency securities (Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac)
- Some states also allow state/municipal bonds

**Crypto-backed reserves are generally NOT accepted as permissible investments.** ForgePay must maintain a separate fiat reserve for outstanding customer obligations.

### Surety Bond Strategy
- Engage a surety bond provider early (e.g., Travelers, Zurich, Markel)
- Multi-state bundling may reduce premium costs
- Bond amounts may need to increase as ForgePay's volume grows — monitor quarterly

### Estimated Total Cost — Tier 1 and Tier 2 (9 states)
- Surety bonds: ~$3,000,000–$4,000,000 in bonded coverage (varies by premium)
- Application fees: ~$25,000–$35,000
- Legal/consulting fees: ~$150,000–$250,000
- Ongoing annual renewal fees: ~$25,000–$30,000/year
- NY BitLicense: separate — see `05_ny_bitlicense_package.md`
